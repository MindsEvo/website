2. Qd7+: The queen controls the seventh rank and cuts off the route upward, forcing the king toward a6.
3. Qb5+: The queen checks along the diagonal and drives the king to a7 near the edge.
4. Ra1+: The rook controls the a-file and completes the mating net.
