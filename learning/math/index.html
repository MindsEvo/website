数学启智 Math Thinking

以数学为载体，在探索与练习中培养孩子的核心思维能力。 Using math as a medium to build core thinking skills through exploration and practice.

比较Comparison

从大小、多少、长短到数量与关系，在比较中发现差异、理解关系。From size and quantity to relationships — discovering differences and understanding connections through comparison.

数感Number Sense

感受数量、大小、距离与变化，逐步建立灵活而直观的数感。Develop an intuitive feel for quantity, magnitude, distance, and change.

规律Pattern

从数字、图形、颜色与变化中发现规律，尝试预测下一步。Spot patterns in numbers, shapes, colors, and changes — then predict what comes next.

建模Modeling

把生活中的问题转化为图形、数量和关系，用简单模型理解世界。Turn real-world situations into shapes, quantities, and relationships — understanding the world through simple models.

逻辑Logic

通过分类、排序、条件和排除进行推理，学会有依据地判断。Reason through classification, ordering, conditions, and elimination — learning to reach conclusions with evidence.

策略Strategy

面对不同选择，尝试规划、预测和调整，寻找更好的解决办法。Plan, predict, and adjust when facing choices — finding better solutions step by step.
