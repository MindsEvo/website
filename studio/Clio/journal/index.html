在一个普通的周末里，Clio 以 MindsEvo 创意工作坊首位小创作者的身份加入了这次共创。我们一起完成了 3 个原创创意设计和 4 个互动网页游戏。在整个过程中，Clio 深度参与了想法讨论、设计表达、实现沟通、试玩验证和持续改进。这两天也成为创意工作坊理念第一次完整落地的样本。 During an ordinary weekend, Clio joined MindsEvo Creative Workshop as its first young creator. Together, we completed three original creative designs and four interactive web games. Throughout the process, Clio actively participated in brainstorming, design, implementation discussions, testing, verification, and improvement. These two days became the first complete example of the Creative Workshop concept.

难忘时刻Memorable Moments

三个值得记住的瞬间Three Moments Worth Remembering

“这是第几个‘马上’了？”"Which 'soon' is this?"

在等外公做饭的时候，Clio 一直追问什么时候开始开发，还开玩笑地数外公到底说了几次“马上”。While waiting for Grandpa to finish cooking, Clio kept asking when development would begin and jokingly counted how many times Grandpa had said "soon."

该回去继续干活了Time to Go Back to Work

在游乐场休息之后，Clio 主动提出：After taking a break at the playground, Clio suggested:

“我们该回去干活了。”"We should go back to work."

这不是被催促后的配合，而是真正发自内心的投入。This reflected genuine enthusiasm rather than being asked to continue.

开始理解 MindsEvoUnderstanding MindsEvo

在讨论 logo 和公司名字含义之后，Clio 开始把平台理念带入对新想法的判断里。比如她能意识到“兔子帽子”这个想法在原始形态下更偏娱乐，而不是思维训练。After discussing the logo and the meaning of the company name, Clio began applying the platform philosophy when evaluating new ideas. One example was recognizing that the Rabbit Hat idea, in its original form, was mainly for entertainment rather than thinking training.

导师记录Mentor's Notes

每一个创作者都有独特的思考方式。我们的目标不是判断一个想法好不好，而是帮助每位创作者发现它、发展它，并逐步把它变成现实。Every creator has a unique way of thinking. Our goal is not to judge whether an idea is good or bad, but to help each creator discover, develop, and gradually turn their own ideas into reality.

在这两天里，Clio 不只是做出了几个有趣的游戏，也开始理解创意工作坊的精神: 一起思考、一起讨论、一起改进、一起创造。During these two days, Clio not only created several interesting games but also began to understand the spirit of Creative Workshop: thinking, discussing, improving, and creating together.

这才是她创作旅程真正的开始。This is the true beginning of her creative journey.
