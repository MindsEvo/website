策略大师Strategy Master

策略大师训练的是「决定下一步怎么办」，而不是当下观察或事后回忆。当前开放：资源权衡、路线选择、装包取舍、后果预测与应对变化，更多维度将持续加入。Strategy Master trains deciding what to do next, not ongoing observation or after-the-fact recall. Two dimensions are open now — resource trade-off and consequence prediction — with more to come.

🔋

能量分配Energy Planner

能量有限，自己点选要做的事搭一个计划，提交后马上看到结果：剩多少能量、得到几颗星。不满意可以再想一次。K1 到 G2：从多做几件事，到选出星星最多的组合。Energy is limited. Tap the tasks to build a plan and see the outcome at once: energy left, stars earned. Not happy? Rethink it. K1 to G2: from doing more tasks to earning the most stars.
🛣️

近路还是远路Near or Far

近路短但可能有路障，远路长但更安全。先选一条路，看小动物走过去的结果，再决定要不要换。K1 到 G2：从近与远，到谁更省力，到星星最多，再到藏着陷阱的路。The near road is short but may be blocked; the far one is long but safe. Pick a route, watch the result, and decide whether to change. K1 to G2: near or far, the easiest route, the most stars, then a route hiding a trap.
🎒

背包装什么Backpack

出行前装背包：每类必带的选一样，再用剩下的重量带喜欢的东西。点出发马上看结果，不满意可以重装。K1 到 G2：从装得下，到多带几样，到星星最多，再到三类必带的取舍。Pack for a trip: one item from each must-bring group, then use the weight left for things you like. Press Go to see the result at once; repack if you are not happy. K1 to G2: just fitting, bringing more, earning the most stars, then three must-bring groups.
🔄

规则变了Rule Change

小动物有一个原计划，走到路口，情况变了：路被堵、变难走，或者能量突然少了。原来的路还行吗？要不要换？有时变化跟原计划没关系，坚持才是对的。The animal has a plan; at the junction things change: a road is blocked, gets harder, or energy suddenly drops. Is the old road still fine? Should it switch? Sometimes the change does not touch the plan, and keeping it is right.
🔗

下一步会怎样What's Next

魔法机器的规则卡每题都不一样：按几号会发生什么？反过来想要某件事发生该按几号？高年级还有一件事引起下一件事的连锁。选错了机器会演示实际发生了什么，再试一次。The machine's rule cards change every question: what happens when this number is pressed? Which number makes a wanted thing happen? Older grades add chains where one thing sets off the next. A wrong choice shows what really happens, then you try again.
说明：更多策略维度（如顺序规划、风险权衡）即将上线。 Note: more strategy dimensions (sequencing, risk trade-off) are coming soon.
