Pattern Hunter Pattern Hunter

Pattern Hunter 聚合了所有当前可用的规律训练游戏。你可以从不同维度开始，持续训练观察、推理与综合判断能力。 Pattern Hunter gathers all currently available pattern training games. Start from different dimensions to continuously build observation, reasoning, and integrated judgment skills.

123

数字规律Number Pattern

补出数列中缺失的数。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从 10 以内往后数，到倒着数、2/5/10 跳着数，再到自己算出步长。Fill the missing number in a sequence. Four grades from K1 to G2: counting on within 10, counting back, skip counting by 2s/5s/10s, then working out the step yourself.
👁️

视觉规律Visual Pattern

补出图形序列中缺失的一项。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从两种形状交替、三拍形状，到箭头每次转 90 度、再细到 45 度。Fill the missing figure. Four grades from K1 to G2: two shapes taking turns, three-beat shapes, then an arrow turning 90° and finally 45° each time.
🎨

颜色规律Color Pattern

找出重复的颜色单元并补出空格。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从两色交替、三拍、四拍且空格在中间，到把两个颜色当作一组。Find the repeating color unit and fill the gap. Four grades from K1 to G2: two colors, three beats, four beats with a mid-strip gap, then ordered color pairs.
🏃

动作规律Motion Pattern

预测运动的下一步。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从方向交替、三个动作一组，到在格子轨道上固定步长跳、再到越跳越远。Predict the next move. Four grades from K1 to G2: alternating directions, three-action sets, fixed-length hops on a track, then hops that grow each time.
⏰

时间规律Temporal Pattern

补出时间序列中缺失的一项。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从生活事件的先后，到一天与四季、一星期，再到跨过 12 点的钟面时间。Fill the missing step in a time sequence. Four grades from K1 to G2: everyday event order, times of day and seasons, the week, then clock time across 12.
📏

大小规律Size Pattern

看懂大小变化并补出空缺。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从一大一小交替、逐级变大/变小，到空格在中间、再到大小波浪循环。Read size changes and fill the gap. Four grades from K1 to G2: big-small alternation, one-step growth or shrinkage, a mid-strip gap, then a size wave.
🧭

空间关系Spatial Pattern

Route Scout 路径规划：看地图避障并选择可达路线，训练空间规划能力。Route Scout planning: read maps, avoid blockers, and choose reachable routes to train spatial planning.
🧮

数量规律Quantity Pattern

用实物看数量变化，不出现数字。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从多少交替、一个一个变，到两个两个变、再到三种数量循环。Read quantity changes from objects, no numerals. Four grades from K1 to G2: more/fewer alternation, one at a time, two at a time, then a three-amount cycle.
🧠

逻辑推理Logic Pattern

根据“谁比谁高/快/大”的线索推理。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从一条线索、反过来问，到两条线索找最…、再到三条打乱的线索排名次。Reason from clues about who is taller, faster or older. Four grades from K1 to G2: one clue, asked backwards, two clues for the most/least, then three shuffled clues to rank.
🧩

综合规律Mixed Pattern

同时看懂两个维度的规律。K1 到 G2 四个等级：从一个变一个不变、两个一起交替，到各变各的、再到节奏错开。Read two dimensions at once. Four grades from K1 to G2: one changes, both alternate, two different rules, then offset rhythms.
🕵️

找不同侦探Difference Scout

比较左右图列，锁定唯一差异位置，训练持续视觉搜索与注意力。K1 到 G2 四个等级：格子从 4 个加到 8 个，差异从一眼可辨细到只差一档大小。Compare the two strips and lock the single mismatch, training sustained visual search and attention. Four grades from K1 to G2: 4 slots growing to 8, and differences narrowing from unmistakable to a single size step.
