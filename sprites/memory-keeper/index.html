记忆守护者Memory Guardian

记忆守护者训练的是「看过/见过之后凭记忆回答」，而不是当下持续观察或比较。当前开放视觉再认、空间记忆、顺序记忆与工作记忆加工，更多机制将持续加入。Memory Guardian trains recall after something is gone, not ongoing observation or comparison. Visual recognition, spatial memory, sequential memory and working-memory manipulation are open now, with more mechanisms to come.

🃏

闪现卡片Flash Cards

一组卡片闪现后藏起来，从选项里找出刚才看到的那张。K1 到 G2 四个等级：卡片从两张到五张，展示时间越来越短，干扰项从跨类别到同类别。Cards flash then hide; pick the one you saw. Four grades from K1 to G2: two to five cards, shorter exposure each time, distractors moving from a different category to lookalikes in the same one.
🗺️

宝藏格Treasure Grid

宝藏在格子里短暂出现后藏起来，记住编号再作答。K1 到 G2 四个等级：格子从 2×2 到 3×3，宝藏从一处到两处，藏起来后的等待也越来越久。A treasure flashes in a grid cell then hides; remember its number. Four grades from K1 to G2: grid grows from 2x2 to 3x3, treasures from one to two, with a longer wait before you can answer.
🎬

记住再行动Remember & Act

动作藏起来后再完成。K1 记住一个动作，K2 按顺序重建，G1 反过来做，G2 根据新变化更新原来的动作序列。Complete actions after they hide. K1 remembers one action, K2 rebuilds the order, G1 reverses it, and G2 updates the hidden sequence after a change.
🪄

魔法顺序Magic Sequence

符号一个接一个出现并消失，再按原顺序重建。K1 从两个符号开始，G2 还要完成一次指定替换。Symbols appear one at a time and disappear, then rebuild their order. K1 starts with two symbols, while G2 also applies one replacement.
🧩

记忆配对Memory Pairs+

先观察全部卡片，藏起来后凭位置记忆完成所有配对。K1 从两对开始，G2 还要更新两张交换位置的卡片。Preview every card, then complete all matches from location memory after they hide. K1 starts with two pairs, while G2 also updates two swapped positions.
🖼️

发生了什么What Happened?

先记住原来的小场景，场景隐藏后会带着一个变化回来。K1 找消失的对象，G2 判断对象移动或替换。Remember the original scene, then identify the change when it returns. K1 finds a missing object, while G2 distinguishes movement from replacement.
🔎

记忆侦探Memory Detective

线索短暂出现后藏起来，保持并合并动物、颜色和随身物品线索，锁定唯一角色。G2 还会更新一条隐藏线索。Clues appear briefly and hide. Retain and combine animal, color and accessory clues to identify the unique suspect. G2 also updates one hidden clue.
说明：对象配对、情境变化与记忆侦探机制已加入，Memory Guardian 第一批与第二批代表机制均已形成可玩的 K1-G2 MVP。 Note: Object pairing, contextual change and Memory Detective are now included. The first and second groups of representative Memory Guardian mechanisms now have playable K1-G2 MVPs.
